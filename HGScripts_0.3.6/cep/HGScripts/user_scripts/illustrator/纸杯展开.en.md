# Paper Cup Template

## Purpose

Enter the top diameter, bottom diameter, and either actual height H or slope length S to create an editable cup-body template for artwork or a printed paper mock-up.

Supports an ideal straight-sided truncated cone, or a cylinder with equal top and bottom diameters. The top diameter must be at least the bottom diameter; both diameters and the height must be greater than zero.

## How to Use

1. Run `纸杯展开.jsx` in Illustrator to open the persistent window. You can keep working on the canvas while it remains open. Running the script again shows the existing window.
2. On the Dimensions tab, enter **A top diameter**, **B bottom diameter**, and either **H actual height** or **S slope length**, in millimeters. H is the vertical distance between the two measured circles; S follows the sloping wall. Editing H calculates S, and editing S calculates H. The last height field you edit remains the input when you later change either diameter; the other height is recalculated. Measure the same section of the cup and exclude the rolled rim and bottom foot.
3. Set an overlap width and choose Left or Right. The default width is `0`, with no overlap. Set the bleed width if needed; bleed offsets the body boundary and **does not wrap around the overlap's outer edge**.
4. On the Guides & labels tab, choose the division counts, reference lines, circle center, body center cross, dimensions, and template notes. Type a division count or use the ▲ / ▼ controls to its right to add or subtract 1. These options are independent.
5. Activate the target document and artboard, then check Preview to draw a temporary template at actual size. Edits update it; turning Preview off or closing the window removes it. To preview, open a document first. Generate can create a new CMYK document when none is open.
6. Click Generate template to keep a final template. The window stays open for the next one. Later preview edits do not modify generated templates. Numbers accept decimals, full-width characters, and an optional `mm` suffix, such as `80.5`, `８０.５`, or `80.5 mm`.

The finished cup and flat sheet drawings beside the form explain the dimensions; the actual preview is drawn on the canvas. Green **H actual height** is the vertical distance on the upright cup. Orange **S slope length** follows the sloping wall and is measured between the inner and outer arcs on the flat sheet. For example, top `90 mm`, bottom `60 mm`, and H `100 mm` give S of about `101.12 mm`, so the sheet measurement should not be compared directly with H. When the diameters are equal, H and S are equal. An entered S must be greater than half the diameter difference to form a body with positive actual height. Invalid or incomplete dimensions show a message and remove the outdated preview.

The window uses the HGScripts panel language selected when it opens, in Chinese or English.

## Guides and Dimensions

- **Angular divisions:** divide the sector angle equally, creating radial dashed segments within the body. Equal diameters divide the rectangle's width into vertical strips.
- **Wall divisions:** use equal distances along S, creating concentric dashed arcs. Equal diameters divide the rectangle's height into horizontal strips. These are equal distances, not equal areas.
- Both counts accept whole numbers from `1–100`. `1` adds no division lines; `4` adds `3` interior lines. The ▲ / ▼ controls stay within that range and update the artwork when Preview is checked. Overlap and bleed do not affect these divisions.
- **Circle center O:** marks the common center of the two arcs, which may be outside the artboard. This option is unavailable for equal diameters. Near-equal diameters can put the center beyond the supported range; the template remains, with the marker omitted and a status message.
- **Body center cross:** marks the midpoint between the arcs on the existing center line with a solid cross. It excludes overlap and bleed, and is distinct from circle center O.
- **Dimensions:** labels the top arc length, bottom arc length, and slope length S. Arc lengths exclude overlap. For equal diameters, the top and bottom edges show the corresponding circumference.
- **Center dashed line** and **template notes** have independent switches. The notes still list H and S.

## Result

- Creates an editable, closed black outer cutting outline on a new, separate layer, centered on the active artboard.
- Each template has one parent layer with real sublayers for the cutting outline, artwork area, bleed, overlap seam, radial divisions, concentric divisions, center dashed line, body center cross, circle center O, dimensions, and template notes. Only categories used by this template receive a sublayer. You can hide or lock these separately. Temporary previews use the same structure.
- The **artwork area** is always generated as a separate closed path for the body, excluding overlap and bleed: an annular sector for a truncated cone, or a rectangle for equal diameters. It has no fill and a thin solid blue stroke on its own Artwork area sublayer. Select it for layout alignment or use it as a clipping-mask path yourself; the script does not create a mask or modify existing artwork automatically. It remains a separate path even when overlap is `0` and it coincides with the cutting outline.
- With overlap enabled, the outer cutting outline includes the overlap. The original side seam is shown as a blue dashed line.
- A separate magenta dashed contour shows bleed. It is a layout reference and does not extend or clip existing artwork automatically.
- The artwork-area outline, dimensions, text, center marks, bleed, and blue dashed lines are references only. Cut along the black cup-body cutting outline, ignoring the exterior dimension lines.
- A template larger than the artboard is not scaled down automatically. Use a larger artboard for complete output.
- Preview adds and updates real temporary artwork, so it may affect the document's unsaved state and undo history. Turning it off removes this tool's temporary artwork and preserves artwork you added separately, but does not guarantee restoration of the earlier undo history. Keep final artwork outside the preview's parent layer.

## Printing and Making

- Print at **100% / Actual Size** and disable options such as “Fit to Page.”
- Make a paper mock-up and check the measurements and overlap before production. Paper thickness, bonding, and forming processes may require additional allowances.
- Generates the body template only. It does not create the rolled rim, cup bottom, bottom foot, or other manufacturing structures.
